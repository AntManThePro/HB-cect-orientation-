You don’t replace IKECA.
You own the proof they don’t test.

hbacademy.netlify.app is a real field-training system. It is not an industry standard, not a personnel certification, and not something a fire marshal is required to accept. That is the starting condition — not an insult.

NFPA 96 already owns fire protection and “trained, qualified, certified person acceptable to the AHJ.” ANSI/IKECA C10 / I10 / M10 already own cleaning, inspection, and end-user maintenance methodology. IKECA CECT / CECS / CESI already own the written credential most AHJs recognize. Fighting those documents is how you lose a decade. Filling the hole they left is how you become unavoidable.

What IKECA exams test

100-item multiple choice. Codes, components, chemicals, solid fuel, safety theory. Pass 70% (CECT) or 75% (CESI). Two hours. English/Spanish. Proctored online.

What they do not test

Before/after photo pairs. Timestamp integrity. Photo reuse. Fan hinge proof. Roof contamination. QA scoring of a live job package. Field sign-off gates. Fraud patterns.

What Hood Boss already built

Path-specific academy (L1–L3 + cert readiness). Photo discipline. Documentation habits. QA inspection logic. Field sign-off. That is the gap product. Name it HB-Q10 and stop calling it “the test that should replace theirs.”

Five tracks. Run them in parallel.

Standards are political machines with checklists. You do not “submit the test.” You occupy every layer that currently decides what a finished hood job looks like.

TRACK 1 — Company law · week 1

Make HB-Q10 the only acceptable package inside Hood Boss. Academy completion required before unsupervised work. QA Brain scores every job. Incomplete package = job not closed, not “we’ll coach later.” This is how a standard is born: someone with payroll power refuses to pay for missing proof.

TRACK 2 — Buyer / insurer gravity · 30–180 days

A standard becomes real when a check writer requires it. Target: 3 restaurant groups, 1 insurance loss-control contact, 1 GC that specs hood work. Language: “Vendor shall submit an HB-Q10 evidence package (or equivalent photo/timestamp schema covering the 15 required categories).”

TRACK 3 — IKECA insertion · 3–18 months

Do not ask them to retire CECT. Ask for: (1) Academy modules listed as CEU-eligible for renewal, (2) Strategic Partner / Education Committee presentation on field-evidence failures CECT does not catch, (3) joint language that CECT = knowledge, Q10 = proof. Use the existing My Tech Pix partner channel. Email: info@ikeca.org + jeff@thehoodboss.com.

TRACK 4 — Accredited certificate program · 12–24 months

ANSI/ASTM E2659 via ANAB is for training certificates, not “this person is a certified technician.” Requirements: program already running with real learners, item analysis, learning objectives, evaluation data. You cannot accredit a slide deck. Pilot N techs, keep scores, then apply. This lets you say “ANAB-accredited certificate program” — still not ISO 17024 personnel cert.

TRACK 5 — Personnel cert or new ANS · 2–5 years

ISO/IEC 17024 personnel certification body: job analysis, impartial scheme committee, exam security, appeals, surveillance. Cost band commonly $15k–$40k+ accreditation plus scheme build; 12–18 months after the program is already live. Creating a new American National Standard requires becoming an ANSI-Accredited Standards Developer first (~$5k application + membership + procedures + balanced consensus body). Only do this for a new document titled like “Field Evidence Requirements for Commercial Kitchen Exhaust Cleaning.” Never a rival C10.

HB-Q10 draft — the actual test

Full text ships in HB-Q10-DRAFT.md. This is the kernel the Academy must grade against.

15 required photo categories

  1. Arrival / protection
  2. Hood before
  3. Filters before
  4. Duct before
  5. Fan before
  6. Roof before
  7. Hood after
  8. Filters after
  9. Duct after
  10. Fan after
  11. Roof after
  12. Panels labeled
  13. Cleanup
  14. Deficiencies or written none
  15. Sticker / tag

Integrity fails

Clock drift. After-before sequence. Recycled photos. 25 shots in 5 minutes. Missing fan. Missing pair. Grease left in customer area. Unsafe ladder. Any one of these is not a coaching moment. It is an incomplete job.

Score weights

Safety 30 · Documentation 30 · Quality 25 · Compliance 15.
90+ Approve · 80–89 Coach · 70–79 Review · 50–69 Reject · <50 Escalate.

Levels vs IKECA

NFPA 96 and ANSI/IKECA C10 define what “clean” means. CECT tests whether a person can talk about that correctly. Callbacks, insurance disputes, and AHJ distrust are now driven by something else: job packages that cannot prove the work.

AHJ / Fire Marshal (local pilot)

Subject: Request to pilot an inspectable KEC evidence package in [jurisdiction]

NFPA 96 requires cleaning by a trained, qualified, certified person acceptable to the AHJ. We are not asking you to invent a new credential.

We are asking you to look at 10 real job packages produced under HB-Q10 (15 photo categories, timestamp rules, deficiency tags, QA score). If the package lets your inspector confirm access was opened, the fan was cleaned, and deficiencies were written down in under two minutes, we will keep using it. If it does not, we will change it.

This is offered as evidence of workmanship, not as a substitute for IKECA or for your authority.
