# HB-Q10 — Field Evidence, Photo Discipline, and QA Sign-Off Standard
## Draft 0.1 for Industry Circulation
### Kitchen Exhaust Cleaning (KEC) — Complements NFPA 96 and ANSI/IKECA C10 / I10 / M10
**Status:** Company-controlled draft. Not an American National Standard. Not IKECA-endorsed.  
**Purpose:** Close the operational gap those documents leave open: *how a crew proves the work happened, how a QA reviewer scores the package, and how a buyer or AHJ can reject a fraudulent or incomplete job without arguing taste.*

---

## 0. Relationship to existing standards (non-negotiable)

HB-Q10 does **not** replace:
- NFPA 96 (design, fire protection, frequency, “trained / qualified / certified person acceptable to the AHJ”)
- ANSI/IKECA C10 (cleaning methodology and bare-metal outcome)
- ANSI/IKECA I10 (inspection methodology)
- ANSI/IKECA M10 (end-user interim maintenance)
- OSHA, SDS, equipment manuals, company SOPs, or local AHJ orders

HB-Q10 is a **field-evidence overlay**.  
If C10 says “clean to bare metal,” Q10 says “here is the photo set, timestamp rule, deficiency tag schema, and QA score that prove it.”

---

## 1. Scope

Applies to Type I commercial kitchen exhaust systems cleaned or inspected as a contracted service:
- Hood canopy and filters
- Ductwork and access panels
- Exhaust fan (hinge kit, blades, housing, curb, stack)
- Roof / discharge area
- Fire-suppression visual condition notes (not a 17A inspection)
- Customer-area protection and post-job cleanup

Out of scope: residential systems, HVAC, suppression system *testing*, design/engineering.

## 3. Minimum evidence package (the test)

A job is **incomplete** — not “needs coaching later” — if any required category is missing.

### 3.1 Required photo categories
1. Arrival / site condition (kitchen floor, customer area protection)
2. Hood interior before
3. Filters before (removed, both faces)
4. Duct access opened — interior before
5. Fan hinged / opened — blades and housing before
6. Roof / curb / stack before (if roof-mounted)
7. Hood interior after (same angles as before)
8. Filters after (cleaned or replaced, both faces)
9. Duct interior after
10. Fan after (blades, housing, hinge hardware seated)
11. Roof after + no standing water / no grease tracking
12. Access panels reinstalled and labeled
13. Customer-area cleanup / no overspray
14. Deficiency close-ups (if any exist; “none found” must be written, not implied)
15. Crew + completed hood sticker / service tag in place

### 3.2 Timestamp and integrity rules
- Device clock must be correct to ±5 minutes of local time.
- Sequence must be physically possible (before cannot follow after on the same component).
- Duplicate / recycled photos from prior jobs are an automatic **FAIL**.
- Burst of 25+ photos in under 5 minutes with no component change is a **FRAUD FLAG**.
- EXIF or app-stamped time + GPS (when available) retained with the package.

### 3.3 Notes required
- System type and access limitations
- Components **not** reached and why
- Deficiencies found (code-aware language, not vibes)
- Chemicals used (product name + SDS reference)
- Safety exceptions (ladder, weather, lockout)
- Time on site (start / stop)

---

## 4. Cleanliness outcome language

Align to ANSI/IKECA C10 “bare metal” language for accessible surfaces.  
HB-Q10 adds a **visibility rule**: if a reviewer cannot see bare metal in the after photo at the same angle as the before photo, the component scores as **not proven**, even if the tech “says it was cleaned.”

Unreachable sections must be documented, not hidden.

---

## 5. Scoring model (the actual test)

Four scores, 0–100:

- **Quality** — remaining grease / carbon / damage on hood, filters, duct, fan, roof
- **Documentation** — category coverage, angle match, timestamps, notes
- **Safety** — ladder/roof/electrical/chemical/slip evidence and mitigation
- **Compliance** — NFPA 96 + C10 awareness demonstrated in the package (labels, access, sticker, frequency note)

**Final** = weighted: Safety 30 · Documentation 30 · Quality 25 · Compliance 15

### Verdict bands
| Final | Verdict |
|---|---|
| 90–100 | APPROVE |
| 80–89 | APPROVE WITH COACHING |
| 70–79 | MANAGEMENT REVIEW REQUIRED |
| 50–69 | REJECT AND RETURN TO TECHNICIAN |
| 0–49 | ESCALATE IMMEDIATELY |

Auto-escalate regardless of score if: no fan photos, no before/after pair, missing access-panel proof, visible remaining grease in customer area, unsafe ladder practice, reused photos.

## 7. Assessment design (how “the test” stays defensible)

1. Item bank tied to the 15 photo categories + 10 failure modes.
2. Practical exam = submit a real or proctored job package, scored by a calibrated QA reviewer.
3. Written items only test what field proof requires (not a second CECT).
4. Annual calibration: reviewers score the same 20 gold packages; drift >8 points triggers retraining.
5. Passing practical: Final ≥80 and zero auto-escalate flags.
6. Renewal: 24 months + 8 hours documented field CE aligned to Q10 categories, **or** IKECA CEUs if later accepted.

Psychometrics to add before any ANSI/ASTM E2659 or ISO 17024 filing: job-task analysis, cut-score study, item analysis, appeals policy, impartiality policy.
